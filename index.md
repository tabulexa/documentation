### <br> Why Tabulexa Board?

In mathematics, engineering and other STEM subjects, explanations often develop step by step. Writing during a lecture helps students follow the reasoning and gives them time to take notes.

PowerPoint is useful for presenting diagrams, images and prepared material. However, it offers less flexibility when a lecturer wants to explain a new idea, solve a problem or respond to a question.

Tabulexa Board combines PowerPoint presentations with live handwriting, giving lecturers the flexibility to explain ideas as they develop.

![Illustration of the lecturer and projector views]({{ "/assets/images/combinedwindows.png" | relative_url }})

### Separate views for lecturers and students

A conventional mirrored screen shows students everything the lecturer sees: menus, controls, and changes in zoom and movement. Tabulexa Board separates these roles.

- **Lecturer board:** Your working surface, with pens, controls, navigation and the freedom to zoom and pan.
- **Projector window:** The student-facing presentation, showing a stable full-board view without lecturer-only controls or preview guides.

This matters particularly for handwriting. Text that feels comfortable to write on a pen tablet may be much larger than writing on paper. The lecturer can zoom in for comfortable pen movement, while students continue to see the whole board at an unchanged zoom level.


### Write live without giving up preparation

Some explanations work best when written live, such as answering a question, adding notes, or solving a problem step by step. Tabulexa Board lets lecturers write directly on slides using a pen or mouse.

Other explanations are easier to prepare in advance, especially complex diagrams or long calculations. Tabulexa Board can replay groups of PowerPoint ink strokes one step at a time. When replay mode is enabled, these groups first appear in light gray. Lecturers can reveal them by clicking the corresponding boxes on their screen or using the replay buttons.
