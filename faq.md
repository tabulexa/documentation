## <br> Frequently asked questions

### Do I need PowerPoint to use Tabulexa Board?

No. You can create a blank board and write directly on it without opening an existing presentation.

### Why not simply mirror my laptop screen?

Tabulexa Board gives lecturers and students separate views. You can zoom in or move around your board while students continue to see the full slide.

### Can I save my handwriting?

Yes. You can save your board and handwriting in a PowerPoint file. Ink strokes can also be opened in PowerPoint.

### How does Replay mode work?

You can prepare groups of ink strokes or other animated objects in PowerPoint and reveal them step by step during a lecture.

### Can I use Tabulexa Board for online teaching?

Yes, you can try sharing the projector window in your video-conferencing application. The result depends on the application's screen-sharing support.
